    About Tulay sa Pag-Asenso

    Three offices.
    One bridge to opportunity.

    Tulay sa Pag-Asenso 3 in 1 V2.0 brings the Tech4ED Center, PESO Magsaysay and MPTVI of Bayan ng Magsaysay together, so residents can learn digital skills, find work and earn TESDA certifications in one place.

    At its core is the Tech4ED belief: Ready sa Tech, Ready sa Life. Technological readiness is an essential life skill for education, work and personal growth.

    A little clarity

    Before you take your next step.

    Helpful things to know about the center and this directory.

    What can I use for free at the Tech4ED Center?

    Free services include computer and internet use, printing, scanning and encoding, and guided use of digital tools. Ask the staff about schedules and availability for training and other programs.

    Can you help me with PhilSys, PSA, NBI, SSS, PhilHealth or Pag-IBIG online?

    Yes. The center offers online government services assistance, including PhilSys (National ID) registration, PSA and NBI online applications, SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG online services, and eGov PH app assistance. Bring the documents the agency asks for.

    How do I contact MPTVI about skills training?

    Call or text the MPTVI Training Center at 0938-198-3735, or email mptvimagsaysay@gmail.com. Courses include SMAW, Electrical Installation and Maintenance, Heavy Equipment Operator, Driving, Visual Graphic Design, Bookkeeping and Housekeeping, plus Flower Arrangement and Table Skirting.

    Can I submit an application on this website?

    No. This website helps you find programs and where to go. Applications are handled by the Tech4ED Center, PESO or MPTVI directly. Ask them about availability, requirements and how to apply.

    Where can I see current openings and schedules?

    Check the official PESO Magsaysay Facebook page for announcements, or visit the Tech4ED Center. Confirm the details before preparing or submitting documents.
